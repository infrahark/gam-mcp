# Privacy Policy

**gam-mcp** is an open source MCP server that connects Claude to Google Ad Manager.

## Data Collection

gam-mcp does not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers. All data stays on your machine.

## What is stored locally

When you authenticate, an OAuth token is saved to `~/.gam-mcp/tokens.json` on your local machine. This file is never transmitted anywhere except directly to Google's OAuth servers to refresh your session.

## Google's services

gam-mcp uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate with the Google Ad Manager API. Your use of Google's services is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
